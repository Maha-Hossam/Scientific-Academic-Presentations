# Ogbanje: From Myth to Molecular Medicine

## Overview

An academic presentation exploring the historical and cultural context of **Ogbanje**, followed by an introduction to **Sickle Cell Disease**, its biological basis, and its connection to malaria.

The presentation covers:

* The history and origins of the Ogbanje concept.
* Common beliefs and narratives associated with Ogbanje.
* The relationship between sickle cell trait and malaria.
* Current approaches to the treatment and management of Sickle Cell Disease.
