# Scientific-Academic-Presentations

This repository contains a collection of academic presentations developed during my undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Science, Cairo University.

## About

These presentations cover different scientific and interdisciplinary topics that I explored as part of my academic learning and university activities.

The repository reflects my experience in:
* Researching scientific topics and reviewing relevant information.
* Organizing scientific content into clear and simple presentations.
* Communicating technical concepts to an audience.
* Exploring interdisciplinary applications of a variety of scientific concepts.
* Developing scientific communication and presentation skills.

## Contents

It includes:
* Presentation visual slides.
* Supporting materials.
* Some presentation recordings.
* Related resources and references.

## Topics

The presentations cover topics from different areas of science, including biophysics, biomedical concepts and their applications, and interdisciplinary technologies, including trending concepts such as digital twins.
