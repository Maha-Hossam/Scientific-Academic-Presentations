## Exploring the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 🔬💡

### About the Presentation

This is a scientific presentation exploring the discoveries behind the **2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine**, awarded to **Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel** for their discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics.

The presentation introduces the story behind the Nobel Prize, the scientists who contributed to this breakthrough, and how their discoveries transformed our ability to investigate neuronal activity and understand the relationship between brain circuits and behavior.

My contribution focused on building the scientific background, explaining the experimental reasoning behind studying neural circuits, and engaging the audience with a question about the role of light in controlling neurons.

## Topics Covered

### 1. The Story Behind the Nobel Prize
- A brief introduction to Alfred Nobel and the origins of the Nobel Prize.
- The purpose of the Nobel Prize in recognizing scientific discoveries and their impact.

### 2. The 2026 Nobel Laureates
- Introduction to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel.
- Their individual contributions to the discoveries behind optogenetics.
- How their work connected light-sensitive proteins with the study of neuronal activity.

### 3. The Science Behind the Discovery
- The interdisciplinary nature of optogenetics, connecting neuroscience, molecular biology, biophysics, and electrophysiology.
- The structure and function of neurons and their electrical signaling mechanisms.
- The complexity of neuronal membranes, ion channels, and membrane potential.

### 4. Correlation vs. Causation in Neuroscience
- Why observing neuronal activity during a behavior is not enough to prove that those neurons cause the behavior.
- How experiments using animal models, including mice, can help researchers investigate the causal relationship between specific neural circuits and behaviors such as fear.
- How optogenetics enables researchers to manipulate selected neurons and test their functional roles.

### 5. The Complexity of Neuronal Control
- The structural and functional complexity of neurons.
- Why selectively activating specific neuronal populations is a challenging scientific problem.
- How the development of optogenetics helped researchers overcome limitations of earlier experimental approaches.
