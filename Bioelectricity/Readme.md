# The Discovery of Bioelectricity

## Overview

An academic presentation exploring key historical discoveries that contributed to our understanding of **bioelectricity** in living organisms.

The presentation covers:
* Early observations of electrical phenomena and the use of electric fish in ancient Egypt
* Luigi Galvani's experiments and the concept of animal electricity
* Alessandro Volta's interpretation of Galvani's experiments
* The development of the Voltaic Pile
* Early investigations into electrical phenomena in plants
* Electrical signaling in the Venus flytrap
* The role of electrical signals and ion channels in plant physiology
