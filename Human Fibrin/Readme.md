# Human Fibrin: How Protein Architecture Controls Blood Clotting

## Overview

A collaborative academic presentation exploring how the **structure and architecture of human fibrin** contribute to its role in blood clot formation.

The presentation covers:

* The physiological role of fibrin in blood clotting
* Fibrin composition and polypeptide chain organization
* Primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary structure
* The relationship between protein structure and function
* Structural features including alpha helices, beta sheets, and coiled-coil motifs
* Bonds and interactions stabilizing fibrin structure
* Fibrin polymerization and fiber formation
* Protofibril formation, lateral aggregation, and cross-linking
* The role of Factor XIIIa in clot stabilization
